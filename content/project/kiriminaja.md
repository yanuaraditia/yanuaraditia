### Project Initiation (November 2020):

I started the KiriminAja Core Engine project in November 2020 as the first team member. The initial goal was to create a robust logistics platform that could efficiently connect shippers and carriers in Indonesia.

### Team Formation and Growth:

As the project gained traction, I assembled a passionate team of engineers and innovators. Together, we focused on building, developing, and maintaining the platform to ensure reliability and scalability.

### Microservices Architecture:

We adopted a microservices approach, breaking down complex logistics and payment processes into independent, manageable services. Each microservice handles a specific domain, such as shipment management, partner integration, payment processing, or notification delivery.

### Technology Stack:

- **Go:** Used for high-performance backend services.
- **Laravel:** Chosen for rapid API development.
- **Google Cloud:** Provides scalable and resilient infrastructure.

### Benefits of Our Approach:

- Independent deployment and scaling of services.
- Quick integration with new logistics and payment partners.
- High throughput and resilience to failures.

### Integration Achievements:

We have integrated with over 15 logistics partners and more than 5 payment service providers, enabling seamless management of shipments and transactions for our users.

### Continuous Improvement:

The team is committed to continuously adding new features and enhancements to support our clients’ business operations.

### Ongoing Support and Impact:

From system architecture design to deployment and support, we strive to deliver a platform that drives efficiency and growth for businesses across Indonesia and beyond.
